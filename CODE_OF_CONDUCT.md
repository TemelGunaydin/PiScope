# Code of Conduct

Everyone participating in PiScope issues, reviews, and discussions should:

- Be respectful and give constructive, specific feedback.
- Welcome different experience levels and communication styles.
- Critique ideas and code without personal attacks, harassment, or discrimination.
- Respect privacy. Do not share another person's personal information or secrets.
- Avoid spam, threats, and unwanted contact.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, close discussions, or
restrict participation. Responses should reflect the severity and repetition
of the behavior, and may range from a reminder to removal from the project.

For a concern, contact a maintainer through a private channel listed on their
GitHub profile. If none is listed, open an issue requesting a private contact
without including personal details. Do not contact someone who is the subject
of a report if another maintainer is available. This policy covers project
spaces and people representing the project publicly.
